## 👨‍💻 About This Repository

This repository documents my structured **45-Day WordPress Website Development, Search Engine Optimization (SEO), and Digital Marketing Master Course**.

My goal is to master how high-performing websites are architected from scratch, customized with themes/plugins and PHP hooks, optimized for Google Core Web Vitals & Search Rankings, and deployed to grow real-world businesses online. Here, I share my day-by-day visual notebook blueprints, technical workflows, SEO audit checklists, and live reference projects.

## 🎯 What You'll Learn

### 🌐 1. WordPress Website Development & Architecture
- **Foundations & Hosting:** CMS Architecture, Domain, Cloud Hosting, DNS (`A`, `CNAME`, `TXT`) & Free SSL (`HTTPS`)
- **Core Setup:** LocalWP & cPanel Installation, Dashboard Settings, SEO-Friendly Permalinks (`/%postname%/`)
- **Content & Design:** Posts vs. Pages Architecture, Gutenberg Block Editor, Synced Patterns, Categories & Tags
- **Visual Page Building:** Lightweight Themes (Astra, Kadence, GeneratePress) & Elementor Pro Landing Pages
- **E-Commerce Mastery:** WooCommerce Store Setup, Variable Products, Tax/Shipping Zones & Payment Gateways (Razorpay/Stripe)
- **Custom Development:** HTML5, CSS3 (DevTools), PHP Basics for WordPress, Child Themes (`style.css` & `functions.php`), WordPress Loop, Hooks (`add_action` & `add_filter`) & Custom Post Types (CPTs)
- **Performance, Security & Ops:** WebP Image Optimization, Page Caching (LiteSpeed/WP Rocket), Cloudflare CDN, WAF Security (Wordfence), Off-Site Backups (UpdraftPlus) & Zero-Downtime Server Migrations

### 🔍 2. Search Engine Optimization (SEO)
- **Search Engine Mechanics:** How Googlebot Crawls, Renders, Indexes, and Ranks Web Pages
- **Keyword Strategy:** Seed vs. Long-Tail Keywords, Search Volume, Keyword Difficulty (KD) & 4 Search Intents
- **On-Page SEO:** Magnetic CTR Meta Titles (`<60 chars`), Meta Descriptions (`<155 chars`), H1–H6 Hierarchy & URL Slugs
- **Topical Authority:** Pillar Pages, Topic Clusters, Bidirectional Internal Linking & Orphan Page Audits
- **Technical SEO & Schema:** `sitemap_index.xml`, `robots.txt`, Canonical Tags, 301 Redirects & JSON-LD Rich Snippets (FAQ, Product, LocalBusiness, Person Schema)
- **Core Web Vitals (CWV):** Optimizing Largest Contentful Paint (`LCP <= 2.5s`), Interaction to Next Paint (`INP <= 200ms`) & Cumulative Layout Shift (`CLS <= 0.1`)
- **Off-Page & Local SEO:** High-DR Backlink Strategies, Linkable Assets (Free Web Tools), NAP Consistency & Google Business Profile (Map Pack Top 3)

### 📈 3. Google Tools & Digital Marketing
- **Google Search Console (GSC):** DNS Verification, Sitemap Submission, URL Inspection, Crawl Error Fixes & Low-Hanging Keyword CTR Optimization
- **Google Analytics 4 (GA4):** Event-Based Tracking, Realtime Monitoring, Organic Traffic Acquisition & Lead/Purchase Conversion Funnels
- **Google Business Profile (GBP):** Local Map Pack Ranking, Primary Categories, Geo-Tagged Media & Review Management
- **Performance & Research:** Google Keyword Planner, Google Trends, PageSpeed Insights, CrUX Reports & Screaming Frog Audits
